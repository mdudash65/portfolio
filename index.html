ASSESSMENT APPROACH

Clarity before intervention.

01 · Understand the system

Map component responsibilities, important data flows, operational dependencies, and the actual business objective.

02 · Investigate the risks

Correlate runtime evidence, tests, configuration, and targeted code inspection, especially at authorization, persistence, concurrency, and recovery boundaries.

03 · Recommend proportionately

Distinguish confirmed findings from hypotheses. Explain business impact, realistic remediation options, confidence, and effort.

Engineering principle

Investigate the boundaries. Challenge the assumptions. Inspect the implementation. Follow the evidence.
